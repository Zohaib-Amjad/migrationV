About Us

A consultancy built
on the honest answer

HOF Migration advises on skilled migration, study, family reunification and investor routes from Dubai — and turns down the cases it cannot genuinely help with.

Founded 2020 Dubai, UAE ICCRC & MARA certified

Migration advice that starts with what you do not qualify for.

HOF Migration is a Dubai consultancy that takes migration cases from the first honest assessment through to the year after a decision lands. We are ICCRC and MARA certified, and we work on Canada, Australia, the United Kingdom, the United States and Europe.

The firm was built around one observation: most people do not fail because they were ineligible. They fail because nobody told them, early and plainly, which route was theirs — and because the file that went in did not say what it needed to say.

So the first conversation is an assessment, not a pitch. If the answer is that you should wait six months, sit a test again, or that we are not the right firm for your case, that is the answer you get. It costs us work in the short term and it is the only way this is worth doing.
