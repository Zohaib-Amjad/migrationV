U.S. NIW & Investor Visas

These are petitions, not applications. What decides them is the evidence file and the argument built on top of it — which is why they are drafted like a case rather than filled in like a form.

Self-petition Investor routes Intra-company transfer
01

Visa categories & types

The routes this service covers, grouped the way the authorities group them.

EB

Employment-based petitions

2 routes
EB-2 National Interest Waiver Self-petition that waives the job offer and labour certification, argued on the national importance of your work. No employer needed
EB-1A Extraordinary Ability For applicants at the top of their field, evidenced against a defined set of criteria. Priority category
IN

Investor & business

2 routes
E-2 Treaty Investor Non-immigrant visa for a substantial, at-risk investment in a real operating business, renewable indefinitely. Treaty country
EB-5 Immigrant Investor Permanent residence through a qualifying investment and the creation of ten full-time jobs. 10 jobs
L

Intra-company transfer

2 routes
L-1A Manager or Executive Transfer into a US office of a related company, with a route to EB-1C permanence. Up to 7 years
L-1B Specialised Knowledge Transfer for employees holding knowledge specific to the company’s products or processes. Up to 5 years

How we can help

The same process on every case, from the first assessment to the year after the decision.

Profile assessment

We score your profile the way the authorities will — age, qualifications, work history, language results — and tell you where it actually stands before any fee is discussed.

Destination & province selection

Countries and provinces want different occupations at different times. We match your profile to the places where it is genuinely competitive, not the ones that are popular.

Pathway mapping

You get the routes open to you now, and the ones a single change — a language retake, a skills assessment, six more months of experience — would open next.

Documentation & filing

Assessments, language tests, police clearances, and the file itself: assembled, checked and submitted, with every deadline tracked on our side rather than yours.

After the decision

Landing, obligations, dependants and the route onwards — the part most consultancies stop short of, and the part that decides whether the move actually works.
